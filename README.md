# Rendezvous

## Intended functionality of the web app:
The app offers a set of features aimed at simplifying the process of creating and managing
events. Users can create their own clubs based on their interests. When forming a club, users
can provide essential details, including the club's name, description, and privacy settings,
granting control over membership accessibility. Furthermore, they can designate club
moderators to assist in overseeing the club's activities and membership requests. Users can
also send requests to join clubs of their interest, ensuring that clubs maintain a degree of
control over their member base. Beyond clubs, the platform allows users to organize personal
events, whether it be parties, meetings, or gatherings, with options to specify event details and
invite specific individuals. Additionally, the platform offers search functionalities, enabling users
to discover clubs and events aligned with their interests through various filters. This
comprehensive suite of features aims to cultivate a dynamic and engaging community
experience, promoting interaction and organization among its users.



## General list of things the user can do:
#### 1. Create and manage Clubs:
- Users have the ability to create their own clubs and subsequently become club leaders.
- They can accept or decline users that send requests to join their club
- They can choose club moderators to help manage the club's activities and members.
#### 2. Search for Clubs:
- Users can search for clubs or interest groups using keywords, categories, or filters.
#### 3. Request to Join Clubs:
- Users interested in a particular club can send a request to join it.
#### 4. Create and manage Personal Events:
- Users can organize and schedule their own personal events such as parties, meetings, or
gatherings.
- They can provide event details such as date, time, location, description, and set the privacy of
the event.
#### 5. Create and manage Club Events:
- The club leader can create events for their club.
- They can provide event details such as date, time, location, description, and set the privacy of
the event.
#### 6. Register for Events:
- Users can RSVP for club and personal events.
#### 7. Search for Events:
- Users can search for events based on different filters.
